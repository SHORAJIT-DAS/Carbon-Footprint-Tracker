How We Measure Carbon Emissions

Measuring carbon emissions is not about perfection — it’s about accuracy, transparency, and consistency.

Emission Factors

CarboScope uses standardized carbon emission factors to estimate emissions from everyday activities like transport, electricity usage, food, and consumption.

These factors represent the average amount of CO₂ released per unit of activity (for example, kilograms of CO₂ per kilometer driven).

Data Sources

Emission factors are derived from publicly available and widely accepted datasets, including national grid averages, international climate research, and energy authority reports.

We prioritize transparency over false precision — every estimate is explainable.

Why Estimates Still Matter

While individual behavior varies, averages are powerful. They help identify high-impact areas and guide smarter decisions without overwhelming users with technical complexity.
