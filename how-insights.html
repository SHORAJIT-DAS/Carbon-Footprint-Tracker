Understanding Your Carbon Insights

Numbers alone don’t change behavior. Insights do.

Emission Breakdown

CarboScope breaks down your total footprint into clear categories such as transport, electricity, food, and lifestyle consumption.

This helps you see which activities contribute the most — often revealing unexpected patterns.

Trends Over Time

Tracking emissions over weeks and months highlights whether your habits are improving, stagnating, or worsening.

Small, consistent changes matter more than one-time reductions.

Benchmarks & Context

Your footprint is shown in context — compared to national averages and sustainability benchmarks.

This avoids guilt-driven comparisons and instead encourages informed progress.
